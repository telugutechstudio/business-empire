Privacy Policy

Business Empire · Last updated: 18 October 2026 · తెలుగులో చదవండి

Summary

Business Empire is a free game. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress is saved on your own device.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are kept in your browser's local storage on your phone or computer. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

What is sent to our server (only when you use these features)

  • Random player ID – a random code made by the game (not linked to you). Used to count how many people play each day.
  • Leaderboard and weekly tournament – if you join, the boss name you type, your score and your current game world are shown publicly to other players.
  • Cloud save – if you turn it on, a copy of your game progress is stored under a secret 12-letter code so you can load it on another device. Anyone with the code can load that save, so keep it private.
  • Notifications – if you turn them on, your browser's push address and your game timers (free spin, daily reward, pets) are stored so we can send reminders. Turn them off any time in Settings.
  • Friend challenges – a challenge link contains your boss name and score.

Services we use

  • Netlify hosts the game and stores the server data above (Netlify privacy policy). Like any web server, it may keep basic request logs (such as IP address) for a short time.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts (Google privacy policy).
  • Advertising – if ads are shown in the game, they are provided by Google, which may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can manage this at Google Ad Settings. Watching an ad is always your choice.

Children

The game is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Hard reset to delete your progress on the device and turn off notifications in Settings. To remove your leaderboard name or a cloud save from our server, contact us below with your boss name or cloud code.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/telugutechstudio/business-empire.
